A fruit fly's novelty detector on wind turbine SCADA

The fly decides whether a smell is new in two steps: about 150 projection neurons (PNs) fan out to about 2,000 Kenyon cells (KCs), only the 5 % most excited KCs stay active (a sparse "tag"), and the synapses from the active KCs to one output neuron weaken each time the smell comes back. A tag full of unweakened synapses means "never smelled this". Here the smell is the 10-minute state of a wind turbine, the wiring is the real right mushroom body from the FlyWire connectome, and the question is whether that finds faults early, at what cost, and whether the real wiring beats a random one.
